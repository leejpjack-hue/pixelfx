What is HTML Motion?

A dark teal fight composer and player for plate pack testing: open a fight, tweak, Compose, Play.

No. Live preview is honest about cam and plates. FX appears after Compose, with a clear banner when needed.

What is Beat focus?

A quiet control zone: plate strip, cam, timing, and swap only. Atom Picker stays collapsed.

Where do I start?

Land shows pinned and recent plate packs. One click opens the last fight.

What do the dock buttons say?

Plain English: Compose, Play, Pause, scrub. No cryptic transport shorthand.
